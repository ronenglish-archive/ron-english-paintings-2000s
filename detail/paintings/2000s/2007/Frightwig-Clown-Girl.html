Notes

This work is reproduced on the cover of the exhibition catalogue for Big Picture Pop.

In the Big Picture Pop exhibition catalogue, this work was reported as 66 × 48 inches; this catalogue record retains 48 × 68 inches.

A live model photographed by Ron English is retained as supporting reference documentation for the painting.
