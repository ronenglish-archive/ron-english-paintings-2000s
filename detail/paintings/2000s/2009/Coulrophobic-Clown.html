Notes

In Original Grin, Death and the Eternal Forever, and Status Factory, this work was erroneously reported as 60 × 80 inches.

This work appears in images published with coverage of Immortal Underground at Opera Gallery, New York.

This work appears on Artsy’s show page for Skin Deep: Post-Instinctual Afterthoughts On Psychological Portraiture, presented by Lazinc.

This painting is shown in progress at 1:35 in SAFEWALLS’ Vimeo video SAFEWALLS | WHAT IS SAFEWALLS?.

This composition was later adapted by the artist as a related giclée print with silkscreen gloss on Somerset Enhanced Satin 330gsm paper, published by Lazarides Editions. The Art Collectorz listing describes Coulrophobic Clown as a signed limited edition of 50 measuring 70 × 50 cm, released at £375.
