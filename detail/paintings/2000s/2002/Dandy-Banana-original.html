Literature

Swindle Quarterly (Corte Madera, CA: Gingko Press, n.d.), p. 44. ISBN 1-58423-181-5.

Notes

Used as the cover art for The Dandy Warhols’ Welcome to the Monkey House (2003).

This composition was later adapted by the artist as a related screenprint on paper, published by Popaganda.
