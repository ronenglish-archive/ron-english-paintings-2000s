  • Title: Last Cartoon

Literature

Ron English, Abject Expressionism: The Art of Ron English (San Francisco: Last Gasp, 2007), p. 74. ISBN 978-0867196894.

Ron English, Original Grin: The Art of Ron English (Paris: Cernunnos, 2019), p. 255. ISBN 978-2374950938.

Provenance

Private collection.

Notes

This work references Leonardo da Vinci’s The Last Supper.

The composition incorporates a group of pop-culture and character references, including several Disney characters, Bart Simpson, the characters of the children’s television series Teletubbies, the Big Boy mascot, and Olive Oyl.
